04 About

About the artist

Chris Haa is a songwriter and multi-instrumentalist from Richmond, Virginia, writing epic sword-and-sorcery songs.

He is a founding member of Slough Feg and the former lead guitarist of Heart of Darkness, and he earned his BA in Musicology from UC Berkeley in 2002. His sound draws on the classic heavy metal of Black Sabbath, Judas Priest and Iron Maiden.

Following his 2016 album I Will, he launched the Wizard's Remorse saga, which continues with Wizard's Remorse II: War Machine and Wizard's Remorse III: Fate of the World, both on the way. Every song is built around a story.

On his recent songs, Chris sings all the vocals and plays guitar, bass and piano, every instrument except drums, which are played by Lance Koehler. The one guest vocalist is Nya Powell, featured on Otherfinger.
